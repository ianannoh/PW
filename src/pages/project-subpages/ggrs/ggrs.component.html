FEATURED PROJECT

Ghana National Arms Management Portal

A dashboard with segregated powers, to register firearms of both civilians and law enforcement agencies.

year

2025

Team

3 Engineers

Role

Frontend Developer

OVERVIEW

The GNAPS portal:

  • Allows relevant agencies register, flag and revoke firearm licenses.
  • Segregated user power to ensure oversight and security.
  • Has a separate portal to verify status of existing licenses. Made purposeful for patrol officers.
  • Analytics page that shows number of licenses issued, revoked and flagged firearms in the system.

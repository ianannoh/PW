FEATURED PROJECT

VITALS FIRST

A platform for clinics and doctors to monitor patients vitals (blood pressure & sugar level)

year

2025

Team

4 Engineers

Role

Frontend Developer

OVERVIEW

Vitals First receives data from patients via the First Responder App. Its features include:

  • A dashboard for every clinic that enrolls onto the platform.
  • Admins can monitor vitals of patients from only their clinics.
  • Analytics page that gives an overview of patient and clinic data.
